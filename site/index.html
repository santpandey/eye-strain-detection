Your eyes are

blinkwell is an on-device eye-fatigue monitor. Every ~15 minutes it opens your camera for a 60-second check — blink dynamics, eyelid closure, partial blinks — then tells you when it's time for a break and what to do about it. Nothing ever leaves your machine.

How it works

01

Periodic windows

The camera opens for ~60 s every ~15 min — never continuously, and fully released between windows. Your webcam LED is the honest signal.

02

478 landmarks, on-device

MediaPipe Face Landmarker runs locally; frames are processed in memory and discarded instantly. Only summary numbers are kept.

03

Acts, not just measures

When fatigue signals rise vs. your baseline, blinkwell suggests a 20-20-20 break, palming, or full-blink exercises — timed, guided, optional.

Privacy, by construction

No frames leave the device.Zero network calls for video. The only file written is ~/.blinkwell/history.jsonl — numbers.
Camera is on ~7% of the time.It's opened per window and released immediately — the LED can never silently stay on.
No accounts, no sync.Your baseline and history live in your home directory. Delete the folder, everything is gone.
One honest caveat.The bundled MediaPipe runtime may send anonymous usage-metrics pings to Google — their design, no opt-out, contains no images or input data. Block play.googleapis.com to silence it entirely.
Wellness tool, not a diagnosis.blinkwell nudges better habits; it doesn't detect disease. Persistent symptoms → see a clinician.
