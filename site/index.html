Your eyes are
tired. Prove it.

blinkwell is an on-device eye-fatigue monitor. Every ~10 minutes it opens your camera for a 60-second check — blink dynamics, eyelid closure, partial blinks — then tells you when it's time for a break and what to do about it. Nothing ever leaves your machine.

How it works

01

Periodic windows

The camera opens for ~60 s every ~10 min — never continuously, and fully released between windows. Your webcam LED is the honest signal.

02

478 landmarks, on-device

MediaPipe Face Landmarker runs locally; frames are processed in memory and discarded instantly. Look away mid-check and the clock pauses — only aligned time counts.

03

Acts, not just measures

When fatigue signals rise vs. your baseline, blinkwell suggests a 20-20-20 break, palming, or full-blink exercises — timed, guided, optional.

Commands

blinkwelljust this — it asks once, then monitors: ~60 seconds every ~10 minutes (adjustable)
blinkwell historyshows your past checks and trends in the terminal
blinkwell reportopens a page with charts of all your checks

blinkwell --help lists every option, including how often and how long checks run.

Privacy, by construction

No frames leave the device.Zero network calls for video. The only file written is ~/.blinkwell/history.jsonl — numbers.
Camera is on ~10% of the time.It's opened per window and released immediately — the LED can never silently stay on.
No accounts, no sync.Your baseline and history live in your home directory. Delete the folder, everything is gone.
One honest caveat.The MediaPipe engine may send Google anonymous counters — like which feature ran and how long it took. Your camera frames and results are never sent — they never leave the device.
Wellness tool, not a diagnosis.blinkwell nudges better habits; it doesn't detect disease. Persistent symptoms → see a clinician.
